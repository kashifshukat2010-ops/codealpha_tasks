1. Phishing kya hai?

Phishing ek social engineering hamla hai: attacker aap ko dhoka de kar password, OTP ya bank details nikalwata hai — system hack kiye baghair, insan ko target karke.

Common types: Email phishing (bulk fake emails), Spear phishing (ek specific shakhs ko), Smishing (SMS), Vishing (phone call).

2. Phishing email pehchano — 6 red flags 🚩

1. Sender ka address ajeeb: support@paypa1-secure.com — asal paypal.com nahi!
2. Urgency / darr: "Your account will be BLOCKED in 24 hours!" — jaldi ka pressure sochne nahi deta.
3. Generic greeting: "Dear Customer" — asal company aap ka naam likhti hai.
4. Suspicious links: hover karo — link text "login" ho, asal URL kuch aur. bit.ly jaisay shorteners se hoshyar raho.
5. Attachments: unexpected "invoice.zip" / "receipt.exe" — kabhi na kholo.
6. Grammar / design: tootay-phootay jumlay, blurry logos — professional companies aisi email nahi bhejtein.

3. Fake websites — URL tricks 🔗

Attackers asal jaisi sites banatay hain. Check karo:

  • Spelling: faceb00k.com, g00gle-login.com
  • Subdomain trick: paypal.secure-login.xyz — asal domain aakhir mein hota hai (.xyz yahan asal hai!)
  • HTTPS ≠ safe: lock icon ka matlab sirf connection encrypted hai — phishing sites par bhi HTTPS hota hai!
  • Homograph: аpple.com mein 'a' Cyrillic ho sakta hai — nazar se nahi pakra jata.
Golden rule: link par click karne ke bajaye khud browser mein official site ka address likho.

4. Social engineering tactics 🧠

  • Authority: "Main bank ka manager bol raha hun" — ohda dikha kar darana
  • Urgency: "Abhi karo warna account band!"
  • Curiosity / Greed: "Aap ne Rs. 500,000 jeetay hain!"
  • Trust: dost ya boss ban kar message ("CEO fraud" / BEC attacks)
  • Helpfulness: "IT support hun, apna password verify karein"

5. Bachne ke best practices ✅

1. Links par hover karke asal URL dekho — click se pehle.
2. 2FA/MFA on rakho — password leak bhi ho to account bacha rehta hai.
3. OTP / password phone par kabhi share na karo — bank kabhi nahi mangta.
4. Unexpected attachment na kholo, sender ko alag se verify karo.
5. Shak ho to report karo — company IT / PTA / FIA cybercrime.

6. Real-world examples 🌍

2024 — Bank SMS scam (Pakistan): "Dear customer, your ATM blocked, call helpline" — call karne par OTP manga gaya, account khaali. Lesson: bank kabhi OTP nahi mangta.
2016 — US election spear phishing: targeted emails se campaign accounts compromise huay — ek click ne bara nuksan kiya. Lesson: spear phishing sab se khatarnak hai.
Common — Fake courier SMS: "Your parcel is held, pay Rs. 150 customs: [link]" — link fake payment page kholta hai. Lesson: official app/site khud kholo.
